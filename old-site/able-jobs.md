## About Able Jobs
A skilling platform for private-sector jobs (sales, operations, marketing, analytics, finance…). Backed by Elevation Capital and Y Combinator. 2M+ graduates and early professionals.

## Prologue
Vinit joined as the first full-time product designer and worked directly with the co-founders.

## Problem
Only a few candidates enrolled in a course applied for jobs in the app.

## Project context
The north-star metric was placements. Applications lead to placements, so the project targeted the application rate.
[img] https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/61acfa305e678b4d4360930b/1d2708de-ae0a-44f1-aaef-e497f23ab351/Desktop+-+6.jpg

## Process
Product-data analysis → user research → problem identification → ideation → design → testing → hand-off → iteration.
[img] https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/61acfa305e678b4d4360930b/143410d1-13b5-4814-bb42-5dcce2d1a4bb/Frame+3094+%282%29.jpg

## Phase 1 — Discovering the job section
Research: drop-off users analysed in Mixpanel, then two cohorts interviewed (left in module 1 / left after module 1), 10 interviews each.
[img] https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/61acfa305e678b4d4360930b/b48bc843-9fbd-4f6b-9f28-725904212b2c/Frame+3125+%283%29.jpg
Finding: candidates didn't know the job listing page existed.
Design explorations: introduce jobs on the "Prepare" tab, where users land after finishing the course, without hurting learning.
[img] https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/61acfa305e678b4d4360930b/fb6aed76-670f-4e43-a2cf-bc433af4a9f7/Frame+3126+%281%29.jpg
[img] https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/61acfa305e678b4d4360930b/d5d645c2-3292-4ce3-b439-2ec1f328f868/Frame+3127+%281%29.jpg
Impact: **11% increase from course enrollment to the jobs listing page.**
[img] https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/61acfa305e678b4d4360930b/9e50028b-386e-4ffd-a32d-9b89d59eb905/Frame+3128+%283%29.jpg
(Note: the homepage card says the work raised overall job applications by 11%, while this page attributes 11% to the enrollment-to-listing step. Check which is right before reusing the number.)

## Phase 2 — Working on the second drop-off (listing → job description)
Goal: reduce drop-off between opening the listing and opening a job description.
[img] https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/61acfa305e678b4d4360930b/ea90bd99-16c0-4d64-bf03-4ad1e4b83eb1/Frame+3129+%282%29.jpg
Research: two cohorts of 10 interviews each: knew what they wanted / didn't.
[img] https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/61acfa305e678b4d4360930b/ee758bb7-7e3d-4bd2-9a0b-e0acfa950bac/Frame+3130+%281%29.jpg
Finding: candidates couldn't find desirable jobs or didn't know how to. Users who used search and filters explored more job cards, so the design focused on easier search and filtering.
